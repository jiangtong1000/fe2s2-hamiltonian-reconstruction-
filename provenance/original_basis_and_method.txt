Fe2S2 molecular construction: implemented method and provenance

Molecule and coordinates
  Oxidized [Fe2S2(SCH3)4]2-, charge -2, 186 all-electron electrons.
  geometry.xyz contains the Sharma et al. 2014 SI Table 1 oxidized geometry,
  in Angstrom, as published to 0.01 Angstrom. It is experimentally derived
  with methyl substitution, not the relaxed reduced-cluster geometry.
  sources/geometry_provenance.json records the source, hash and atom map.
  Exact identity with the geometry behind the historical FCIDUMP is unverified.

Parent calculation
  PySCF 2.9.0, Python 3.11.13; high-spin unrestricted BP86.
  S=5, multiplicity 11, PySCF spin=10, Nalpha=98, Nbeta=88.
  TZP-DKH all-electron basis, spherical functions, 662 contracted spatial AOs.
  Explicit exponents/contractions and AO labels: parent/ao_metadata.json.
  Molecular checkpoint: parent/scf.chk. No ECP and no AO functions removed.
  Minimum overlap eigenvalue: 0.0007298614636798928.
  Molecular symmetry disabled; PySCF minao initial guess; ordinary UKS SCF.
  DFT grid level 4; energy convergence tolerance 1e-10 Eh,
  orbital-gradient tolerance 1e-6. Actual submitted implementation is
  build/submitted/run_parent_50704020.py; parent output records SCF iterations.
  The metadata key df_total_energy denotes the DFT total energy; it does not
  denote a density-fitting approximation. The parent uses direct Coulomb ERIs.

Relativity and physical Hamiltonian
  PySCF SpinFreeX2CHelper, approximation='1e', xuncontract=True.
  The one-electron X2C operation internally uncontracts the basis and returns
  hcore in the original 662-AO contracted representation. Saved hcore and
  overlap: parent/hcore_ao.npy and parent/overlap_ao.npy.
  Ordinary Coulomb two-electron integrals; no two-electron picture-change
  corrections. Spin-free scalar relativity is not spin-orbit coupling.
  The exported Hamiltonian excludes the DFT XC potential and any AFQMC
  sum-of-squares one-body adjustment.

Parent natural orbitals and split localization
  Parent natural orbitals diagonalize S^(1/2) (Dalpha+Dbeta) S^(1/2).
  Their AO coefficients are S^(-1/2) times the eigenvectors; occupations are
  descending and the largest-magnitude AO coefficient fixes each real phase.
  Artifacts: parent/mo_coeff.npy, parent/parent_natural_occupations.npy,
  both AO spin densities, and the alpha/beta UKS-to-UNO transformations.
  Occupation boundaries 1.5 and 0.5 produce 88 double-like, 10 single-like
  and 564 virtual orbitals. These thresholds are implementation choices,
  not recovered historical settings.
  Within the double-like space a core-character projector selects 44 F
  orbitals; the remaining 44 valence orbitals form a separate PM block.
  PM localization is separate for core, occupied valence and single blocks:
  Mulliken populations, exponent 2, atomic initialization, max_cycle=150,
  conv_tol=1e-10, conv_tol_grad=1e-6, no imposed molecular symmetry.
  orbitals/selection_report.json records achieved objectives and gradients.
  Orbital-character diagnostics use meta-Lowdin AOs with ANO preorthogonalization.

Core and chemical CAS selection
  Genuine F: Fe/S [Ne] plus C 1s, 44 spatial orbitals; Fe 3s/3p retained.
  Character and semicore checks: orbitals/chemical_review.json.
  CAS(30e,20o): ten Fe 3d-derived, six bridging-S 3p-derived, and four
  terminal-S 3p-derived orbitals directed toward their bonded Fe atoms.
  Selection uses orbital character, including ligand mixing, not energies.
  Initial chemical order follows terminal(2), Fe1(5), bridge(6), Fe2(5),
  terminal(2). Exact historical localized-orbital gauge is not claimed.

Correlated natural orbitals and retained partition
  The separate CAS20 natural orbitals come from the singlet CAS(30e,20o)
  DMRG 1-RDM, not the high-spin UKS density. The provisional direct-AO
  density supplies the production rotation; the production density checks it.
  Full rotations, phases and index maps are in orbitals/transformations.npz,
  orbitals/U_natural_20.npy and orbitals/index_maps.json.
  No CASSCF or DMRG-SCF orbital optimization is performed.
  Final retained order [I34 | K6 | A14 | V564]: 618 orbitals, 98 electrons,
  singlet Nalpha=Nbeta=49. Only F is removed from the full Hamiltonian.
  I and K6 have fixed occupations only in the corresponding trial embeddings.
  V is empty only in those trials; full cross-block integrals are retained.
  CAS14 reduction remains subject to the measured compression assessment.

Integral representation and solvers
  Real float64 spatial orbitals, energies in hartree, chemists' ERIs.
  Direct AO modified Cholesky threshold 1e-9 gives 9218 factors; the 1e-8
  prefix has 7545 factors. This is not auxiliary-basis density fitting.
  Full factors: (gamma,p,q), ERI(pq|rs)=sum_gamma L(gamma,p,q)L(gamma,r,s).
  Physical h1 and scalar include genuine F contributions exactly once.
  Both production CAS Hamiltonians are contractions of that same stored
  full representation; sequential contraction is checked independently.
  DMRG uses block2-mpi 0.5.3 SU2 singlets, with state-specific schedules,
  energies, RDM conventions and provenance saved in each CAS directory.
  CAS14 additionally has an independent PySCF direct_spin0 FCI state/RDM.
  New solver accuracy target is 1e-5 Eh; occupations need separate checks.
  Final acceptance is recorded in validation artifacts and manifest.yaml;
  this method description does not certify unfinished numerical checks.

Historical comparison and phase boundary
  The pinned reference FCIDUMP has zero scalar. Its external core energy
  -4976.26532397 Eh is added only for historical total-energy comparisons.
  That scalar is never transplanted into this molecular Hamiltonian.
  Chemical and sorted-natural-occupation agreement do not establish identical
  Hamiltonians; historical AO/MO coefficients are unavailable for direct overlap.
  Native states, RDMs, index/phase maps and full integrals are preserved for
  later VCE/active-space AFQMC. Backend adaptation and propagation are deferred.
